Hello! I'm Lamisa Salsabil, a Sales Associate and Digital Media professional with a Bachelor's degree in Digital Technology and Emerging Media from Fordham University. I am passionate about delivering exceptional customer service, building strong client relationships, and creating meaningful experiences through effective communication and creative problem-solving.

My professional background combines sales, customer service, digital marketing, public relations, graphic design, and social media management. Throughout my career, I have developed strong interpersonal and organizational skills by working in customer-facing roles, collaborating with diverse teams, and supporting marketing and communication initiatives. I enjoy helping customers, understanding their needs, resolving concerns, and contributing to positive business outcomes.

Alongside my sales and customer service experience, I have worked on branding, content creation, UX/UI design, and multimedia production using industry-standard tools including Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, Canva, CapCut, and Microsoft Office. My experience also includes public relations, influencer research, media monitoring, and digital campaign strategy, allowing me to combine creativity with analytical thinking...

As a graduate of Fordham University, I also gained valuable leadership experience by serving as President of the Arts Club, where I organized campus events, managed creative teams, and increased social media engagement through strategic marketing. These experiences strengthened my leadership, teamwork, project management, and communication skills.

I am fluent in English and Bengali, with conversational proficiency in Hindi and Urdu, enabling me to communicate effectively with people from diverse backgrounds. I am always eager to learn new skills, embrace new challenges, and contribute to organizations that value customer satisfaction, innovation, and collaboration.

Whether you're looking for a dedicated Sales Associate, Customer Service Representative, Digital Marketing professional, or Creative Content Specialist, I am committed to delivering high-quality work, building lasting relationships, and helping businesses grow through professionalism, creativity, and a customer-first mindset.
